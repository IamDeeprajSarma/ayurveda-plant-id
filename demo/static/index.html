Tripura Ayurvedic Plant ID

Take or upload a photo of a leaf or small branch to see the closest matches.

For learning only. Never eat or use a plant based on this app.

1. Add a photo

  • One leaf or a small branch, filling about half the frame.
  • Good light and a plain background work best.

Photos and approximate location may be saved to improve the app.

Prototype built for a final-year project. The model knows a limited set of species and will always give its closest guess, even for plants outside that set.
